# Privacy Policy for Snakey - DOM Eating Snake

Last updated: 2026-05-28

Snakey is a browser extension designed to let you play a visual sandbox game on any active webpage. We take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## What Data We Collect
**Snakey does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing information, or website content.**
- The extension performs DOM scans, coordinate updates, and css animations entirely locally within your active browser tab.
- None of the text, layout structures, images, or elements scanned on the pages you visit are ever recorded, stored, or sent off-device.

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any third-party analytics, cookies, tracker scripts, or external APIs. 

## Data Sharing
Since we do not collect any data, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or processed by third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion
No data is ever retained by the extension. When you close the browser tab or disable the extension, all temporary game state is immediately cleared from memory.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this document on the project's repository.
